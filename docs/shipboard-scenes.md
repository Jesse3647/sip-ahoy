# Quiet sailor close-ups

The Overview sailing banner is a passive portrait, with no links, buttons, venue selector, speech bubbles, ambient effects, or background changes while the app stays open. It shows the selected sailor and up to three crewmates, shown as overlapping, complete silhouettes. On phones, three or four sailors form one staggered group on the deck. The slightly smaller rear sailors stand only 48px farther up the deck; the rear-left sailor sits between the front pair, while the rear-right sailor is offset to the right of the front-right sailor, with explicit rear/front layering rather than separate floating rows; desktop uses a shared row. One- and two-person sailings retain the larger portraits. The existing “Your sailors” section below the banner still selects which sailor is featured. Each expression comes from the existing `sailorReaction` rolling six-hour calculation; the banner does not calculate or imply intoxication.

`SailingScene.tsx` combines the original transparent character sheets with one of six clear cruise backgrounds at its natural scale. `SailorBlink.tsx` adds vector eyelids aligned separately for all six characters and all four expression cells. Existing winking eyes stay closed. The overlay is visible for approximately 130–170ms every 7.4–9.3 seconds, staggered between characters. Bodies, mouths, hats, and background remain completely still. All blinking pauses offscreen or in background tabs, and system reduced motion leaves the open-eye artwork static.

## Background selection

The app has six locally bundled scenes: pool deck, piano lounge, sunset deck, island beach, ship promenade, and moonlit deck. On each opening (including returning to a suspended app), it advances one scene only if at least an hour has passed since the previous opening. Quick reopens retain the current scene and reset that opening timestamp. Time passing while the app remains visible does not rotate the background. The index and last-opened time are cosmetic local preferences, independent of saved journals. Corrupt or unavailable preferences fall back safely.

The five new scenes are original generated 1536×1024 illustrations, converted to quality-85 JPEGs for the offline bundle (about 3 MB combined). They share a soft matte miniature-clay style, rounded shapes, bright cruise colors, sharp scenery and open foreground space for sailors. Each was generated separately with no people, characters, logos or lettering. The prompts described a nautical piano lounge; stern deck at sunset; Caribbean beach with cabanas; a glass-roofed indoor promenade; and a moonlit deck with warm string lights. Original PNGs are retained in ignored `work/scene-art/`; shipped JPEGs are in `public/scenes/`.

## Artwork

`public/scenes/pool-deck.jpg` is an original generated Caribbean pool-deck illustration inspired by Royal Caribbean's **The Lime & Coconut**, including its colorful two-story bar, striped awnings, palms, and ocean railings. Venue reference: [Royal Caribbean, Wonder of the Seas](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-ships/wonder-of-the-seas/restaurants-bars/the-lime-and-coconut), checked November 14, 2026. This is a stylized setting, not a photograph or an exact ship layout.

The 1536 × 1024 image was generated with the built-in image tool, then converted to JPEG at quality 85 (about 505 KB). The original brief requested a softly rounded miniature clay scene, front-facing slightly elevated camera, turquoise water, cream coping, pastel sky, aqua/coral bar, striped yellow awnings, palms, and no text or characters. The app uses the illustration without added blur, desaturation, or zoom. A light edge/top gradient blends it into the banner while keeping the bar, ocean, and pool recognizable. Visible names and mood captions are omitted; accessible figure labels retain that context. Character size is limited by the scene height, leaving 12px above and below the complete sprite even for a single sailor. Each character has a square expression frame that can extend into its neighbor’s space; only the shared scene clips artwork, avoiding vertical cuts through arms or hats. The selected sailor remains included; depth ordering keeps the mobile rear pair behind the front pair. The unused earlier Schooner Bar image is retained in the ignored `work/scene-art/` directory, outside the shipped asset bundle.

The background and character sheets are precached for offline use. No external image requests, video assets, journal fields, or saved preferences are introduced.

## Verification

`tests/browser/characters.spec.ts` checks the absence of banner controls, four-person groups even with larger crews, all character assignments, individual moods, lack of body animation, blink overlays, reduced motion, narrow layouts, and offline background loading. Desktop, phone, and closed-eyelid screenshots support visual review. Existing character, save-feedback, and mobile tests exercise integration.
